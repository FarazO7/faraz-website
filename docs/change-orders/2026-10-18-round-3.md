## 1. Items

### Item 1: the Experience panel follows into Education
- **Root cause (confirmed).** In `components/Experience.tsx`, the right-hand column holds both the experience timeline and the Education block. The left column is a sticky grid item, which stays pinned to the end of its grid area. That area is as tall as the whole right column, Education included.
- **Change.**
  - Education moves into its own `<section id="education">` after Experience, so the sticky parent ends with Healthmug and the panel scrolls away with it. No JavaScript is involved.
  - Education gets the same two columns: "Education" (a section heading) and "Where the foundation was laid." on the left, the 01 and 02 entries on the right. Nothing is pinned.
  - The Domains behaviour is unchanged. Past the last role it already keeps Healthmug's set, so nothing flickers.
  - Each block gets the standard section padding, and the last timeline entry loses its bottom padding (56px). The gaps from Experience to Education and from Education to Skills then match the gaps between other sections.

- Content guard check e ("every education entry has dates") is retired.

### Item 3: "Toolkit" label

### Item 4: new resume
- The attached PDF replaces `public/resume/Faraz-Ali-Product-Manager.pdf`, at the same path.
